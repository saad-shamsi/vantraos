The Meat / Making Component Split

In catering, "Mutton Biryani" is rarely bought from a single vendor. Vendor A supplies the 60kg mutton, while Vendor B cooks it in deegs. Vantra splits any dish line into independent vendor assignments with separate delivery schedules and rates.

Unique to Vantra OS

Delayed Billing Architecture

Vendors send bills days or weeks post-event. Unlike Odoo which forces early closing, Vantra’s Vendor Sheet holds open an "Awaiting Rates" status without stalling your client invoicing or financial visibility.

FIFO Cost Settlement

Real Double-Entry Wallet System

Track real liquid money across bank accounts (Meezan, HBL, UBL), mobile wallets (JazzCash, EasyPaisa, Raast, Sadapay), and office petty cash. Every Rupee movement creates an immutable audit trail.

Atomic Transactions

Dual-Rate Custom Bills (Decor & Extras)

External stage decor, floral arrangements, and sound setups often pass through your company. Vantra records internal cost rates vs. client billing rates with automatic profit markup—while client PDFs strictly hide your margins.

Hidden Margin Protection

Safe Invoice Diff & Resync

Client changes the menu 2 days before the wedding? Changing the client invoice triggers an intelligent diff/merge against the vendor sheet—never wiping out the rates or vendor assignments you already entered.

Optimistic Concurrency

Commission Clawback & Audit Log

Sales rep commissions are linked directly to realized invoice settlements. If a booking is cancelled or adjusted, the system automatically claws back the commission allocation with full audit accountability.
